FAQ

Common Questions

How soon will I get it after ordering?

After your payment is verified we usually send access on WhatsApp in 10-30 minutes, at most within 1-2 hours. Game top-ups take 5-15 minutes.

How do I pay?

Send Money to our bKash, Nagad or Rocket number and write the Transaction ID in the order form. You can also chat with us on WhatsApp first and pay after.

What if something goes wrong during my plan?

You get full support for the whole plan. If there is a problem we fix it or give a replacement. If it cannot be fixed, we refund the remaining days.

Is it on my own email or a shared account?

Each plan says it in its name. YouTube Premium, Spotify, Canva, ChatGPT Personal and others are usually activated on your own email. Some plans are on a shared account, where you get your own profile.

Can I get a service that is not listed?

Yes, we sell almost every kind of subscription. If it is not listed, just ask us on WhatsApp or Messenger.
